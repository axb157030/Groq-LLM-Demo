Beneath the gnarled roots of ancient trees and in the shadows of forgotten stones, dwell the chochlik. These small, hunched creatures stand about a meter tall.
The females of this species are distinct, bearing marsupial pouches much like a kangaroo. They are an industrious race, giving birth to undeveloped infants approximately every six months. These tiny beings spend a crucial month forming within their mother's womb before a six-month period of infancy in the pouch. By the tender age of just four years, they have already reached adulthood. They subsist on hunting and heliciculture.
They are not a race to be underestimated. When the need arises, they show remarkable ingenuity, banding together in massive packs to bring down much larger prey or predators.
